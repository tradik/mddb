What's New in v{{$.Vars.mddbVersion}}

  • Automatic failover: with MDDB_CLUSTER_PEERS and three or more nodes, the nodes elect a leader and replace a failed one. In tests a new leader took writes 2.6–3.7 s after kill -9 of the old one.
  • Collections across node groups: MDDB_PLACEMENT_GROUPS splits nodes into groups, each collection lives in one of them, and any node routes a request to the right group. POST /v1/placement/move moves a collection without losing a write.
  • Vector indexes load from disk: 200,000 vectors with HNSW were ready in 4–10 s instead of 117 s, and IVF, PQ, OPQ and SQ no longer retrain at startup.
  • Reranking: an optional model reorders the best candidates of a search. If it times out or fails, the search returns its own order.
  • Tenant limits: quotas, rate limits and concurrency limits per tenant and per API key, with a Tenants page in the panel.
